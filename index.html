The Opportunity

From service provider to market presence

PTP’s toll processing operation is a machine — consistent, reliable, and trusted by the companies that know you. But the sales side? That’s where the margin lives, and right now it’s invisible to the broader market. Buyers in automotive, data centers, construction, and electronics manufacturing are sourcing from competitors not because they’re better — but because they’re findable.

The Foundation

Decades of demand without a single ad

“Most companies market to survive. PTP has thrived without it. That’s not a weakness to fix — it’s a runway to build on.”

When your competitors are spending six figures on trade show booths and SEO campaigns just to maintain market share, and you’ve been growing on reputation alone, the question isn’t “why haven’t we marketed?” — it’s “what happens when we start?”

The answer: you don’t start from zero. You start from trust. Every new channel, every piece of content, every directory listing lands differently when the company behind it has the track record PTP does.

Growth Vectors

Where the new business lives

The sales side grows by reaching buyers who don’t know PTP exists yet. These are the sectors with active demand and fit for your capabilities:

Automotive & Aftermarket

Car bodies, chassis components, aftermarket panels. Southern California is the aftermarket capital — PTP is already in their backyard.

Data Centers

The buildout is massive and accelerating. Steel framing, enclosures, cable trays. Proximity to the LA data center corridor is an advantage.

Construction & Infrastructure

Structural steel, decking, reinforcement. California’s construction pipeline is enormous and local sourcing is preferred.

Electronics Manufacturing

Enclosures, heat sinks, shielding. Precision slitting and blanking capabilities match the tolerance requirements.

Utility & Energy

Utility beds, solar racking, transformer enclosures. PTP’s own solar facility is a proof point.

Appliance & HVAC

Housings, panels, ductwork components. High-volume flat-roll processing is the core competency.

The Bigger Picture

Building value, not just volume

Whether the path forward is sustained independence, a strategic partnership, or a conversation with a Reliance or Worthington — brand visibility and market reach are the multipliers. A company that’s known is worth more than a company that’s good. PTP is already good. This is the plan to make it known.
