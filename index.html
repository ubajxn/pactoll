The Brief

You’ve never needed marketing.
That’s the whole point.

Pacific Toll Processing has operated for decades on reputation, relationships, and the quality of the work. No LinkedIn page. No directory listings. No trade show appearances. And still — an 11-acre, fully solar-powered, crane-served facility in Carson with 8 overhead bridge cranes, a Braner Class-1 Automotive precision slitting and blanking line, an indoor rail spur, and a roster of long-term customers who don’t leave.

That foundation is the asset. The fact that PTP has thrived without marketing isn’t a gap — it’s proof that once the market knows what PTP does, the conversion rate will be enormous. The product is already proven. The facility is already best-in-class. The team already delivers.

01

The 75/25 problem is a findability problem.

Right now, 75% of revenue comes from toll processing and 25% from steel sales. The goal is 50/50. But PTP’s website doesn’t prominently feature steel sales. There are zero directory listings. No social presence. No trade show presence. No Constant Contact. No content pipeline. Most manufacturers in Arizona and California who need flat-rolled steel don’t know PTP sells it.

The close rate on quoted deals is strong. The bottleneck is entirely upstream: not enough of the market knows PTP exists as a steel supplier, and there’s no system capturing the ones who do find you. Fix the findability gap and the close rate does the rest.

02

This suite is the blueprint.

Every tab in this application is a surface I’ll build and manage. The Dashboard tracks real KPIs — toll/sales ratio, leads, website visitors, directory listings, social followers. The Pipeline shows where deals stall and what fixes each stage. Targets identifies 200+ real companies across 6 sectors. Campaigns maps 30+ marketing programs with budgets, timelines, and conversion paths. Calendar lays out the first 90 days. Intel audits every competitor’s digital presence.

What makes this different from a strategy deck: This is a working system, not a slideshow. The data architecture is the plan. This becomes the marketing layer — the scorecards track real numbers, campaigns have timelines and budgets, and every target account has a comms strategy ready for whoever on the team picks it up.

03

You show them the building.
I make sure they find it.

The spring open house is the anchor event. Drone footage of the facility. ASMR-style videos of Braner lines slitting coil. Customer spotlights from long-term toll clients. LinkedIn campaigns targeting purchasing managers at automotive, data center, and construction companies across California and Arizona. Directory listings in SteelOrbis, Metal Center News, IQS. MSCI membership for benchmarking data. And a redesigned website that makes it clear: PTP sells steel.

This isn’t starting from zero. It’s starting from a position of strength that the market just hasn’t seen yet.
