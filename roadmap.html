Digital Roadmap

90 days to make PTP’s sales operation visible

This isn’t a rebrand. PTP’s identity is earned and established. This is about making the sales side as findable and credible as the toll side already is — starting with the lowest-effort, highest-impact moves.

1

Foundation

Days 1–30

Get the basics right. Everything in this phase is either free or low-cost, and most of it can happen in parallel.

  • Rebuild the website

    Website

    The current site is informational but doesn’t sell. Redesign to lead with steel sales capabilities alongside toll processing. Clear product categories, size/weight specs, real facility photography. Mobile-first, fast-loading.

  • Add lead capture

    Lead Gen

    Smart contact form or chat prompt that identifies the visitor, captures the inquiry (product type, quantity, timeline), and routes follow-up to the right person. Filters out non-commercial retail requests automatically.

  • Claim free directory listings

    Presence

    IQS Directory, Google Business Profile (update), Yelp Business. All free. Add facility photos, equipment specs, service descriptions. Takes a day.

  • Set up LinkedIn company page

    Social

    Professional company page with facility photos, capability summary, and employee connections. LinkedIn is where B2B buyers research vendors — PTP needs to exist there.

2

Content & Credibility

Days 30–60

Start producing content that shows the scale and capability of the operation. Let the facility speak for itself.

  • Facility video production

    Content

    Drone footage of the 11-acre facility. Walk-through of the Braner lines, indoor rail, bridge cranes. ASMR-style close-ups of steel being processed — slitting, blanking, coil handling. This content does the selling.

  • Photography library

    Content

    Professional photos of equipment, product, team, and facility. These feed the website, social media, directory listings, and print ads for the next 12 months.

  • Submit to paid directories

    Presence

    SteelOrbis listing, Metal Center News directory and Buyer’s Guide submission. These are where purchasing managers actively search for suppliers.

  • Begin social posting cadence

    Social

    2–3 posts per week on LinkedIn. Mix of facility footage, product close-ups, team features, and incoming material shots. Show the operation in motion. Extend to Instagram for visual content.

3

Market Entry

Days 60–90

With the digital foundation in place, move into the channels where deals start.

  • MSCI membership + event registration

    Presence

    Join MSCI, register for the 2027 Steel Conference. Access benchmarking data, forecasting tools, and the member directory. Signal to the industry that PTP is playing at the professional level.

  • Trade publication outreach

    Presence

    Contact Metal Center News and Modern Metals about advertising and editorial opportunities. Target the Metal Service Centers quarterly section. Explore feature story angle: solar-powered facility, decades of reputation.

  • Plan the spring open house

    Lead Gen

    April 2027 facility open house for existing customers, prospective buyers, and industry contacts. Facility tours, capability demos, catered event. Use the drone and video content to promote it.

  • Launch “PTP Steel Sales” digital experience

    Website

    Dedicated section or microsite focused entirely on product availability, specifications, and quoting. Separates the sales identity from the toll processing identity without splitting the brand.

After day 90: the steady state

The first 90 days build the infrastructure. After that, it’s about consistency — keeping the content flowing, the listings current, and the relationships growing.

Weekly
  • 2–3 social media posts
  • Respond to directory inquiries
  • Review website analytics
Monthly
  • On-site video/photo session
  • Incoming material documentation
  • Lead pipeline review
Quarterly
  • Publication ad placement review
  • Directory listing refresh
  • Sales/toll ratio tracking
  • Competitor activity check
