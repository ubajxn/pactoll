Precision Coil Slitting

Our Braner Slitter Line is a 70,000-pound x 72″ x 1,000 FPM Automotive Class-1 Double-loop Coated Coil with Inline Surface Inspection Stand, ranging in gauges from 0.013″ to 0.250″, with Turret Head Design. This is Braner's most technologically current line, improving upon those used by the nation's finest automotive industry steel service centers. It is specially designed for surface-critical coated materials.

02

Precision Leveling & Multi-Blanking

Our Braner Multi-Blanking Line is a 60,000-pound x 72″ x 250 FPM Automotive Class-1 precision line which blanks in thickness from 0.015″ to 0.135″. This is a high-speed close-tolerance line capable of converting bare and coated carbon flat rolled products into panel-flat sheets and precision blanks at a rate of up to 100 cycles per minute.

Key Capabilities

9 Flight x 5 Hi Cassette Leveler with inline Slitting Turret Head. Hi-Cyclical rate Electronic AC Servo Feed system combined with programmable air stacker for the flattest, most scratch-free precision tolerance blanks in the region.
