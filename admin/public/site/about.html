Our Story

35+ Years of Steel Processing

Pacific Toll Processing started with a simple idea: give steel buyers and mills a reliable, high-quality toll processing partner on the West Coast. From our first slitting line to the full-service operation we run today, that mission hasn't changed.

What has changed is our capacity. We now operate over 100,000 square feet of indoor, overhead-crane-serviced space on 10.6 acres of land. We've added blanking and leveling capabilities, installed rooftop solar, and expanded into direct steel sales. Through it all, we've stayed privately held, industry focused with a constant attention to the highest industry standards for compliance and safety.

Milestones

How We Got Here

1989
Founded in Carson, CA with first slitting line. A single idea: be the best toll processor on the West Coast.
1990s
Expanded facility footprint, added second slitting line. Built reputation as a reliable partner for mills and distributors.
2000s
Installed Braner multi-blanking line. Became the only mill-qualified automotive steel processor in the Western US.
2010s
Added precision leveling capabilities. Grew to 100,000+ sq ft on 10.6 acres with 8 overhead bridge cranes.
2020s
Installed rooftop solar array. Launched steel sales division. Serving the future of American manufacturing.
