Industry Presence

The places PTP should be — and isn’t yet

Publications, directories, trade events, and catalogs are where buyers go before they pick up the phone. Every competitor on the landscape page is in at least three of these channels. PTP is in zero. Here’s what to fix, in what order.

Not listed

What this costs vs. what it costs not to

The total investment to get PTP listed, registered, and present across every channel on this page is a fraction of what competitors spend annually on a single trade show booth. The cost of staying absent is harder to measure — it’s the quotes never requested, the accounts never opened, and the valuation never realized.
