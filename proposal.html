01 — The Situation

PTP Has the Steel. It Needs the Story.

Pacific Toll Processing has 35+ years of operational excellence, a 100,000+ sq ft solar-powered facility, and the capacity to serve the entire West Coast. What it doesn't have is a modern digital presence that matches the quality of its work.

The current website is outdated. There's no CRM. No lead pipeline. No competitive intelligence system. No content strategy. No SEO presence strong enough for AI-driven search to surface PTP when buyers ask "who can slit my coils in Southern California?"

This proposal covers a full digital build-out and ongoing fractional CMO engagement to fix that — not with a one-time project that gets handed off and ignored, but with an ongoing partnership that keeps the engine running.
