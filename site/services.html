Toll Processing

Processing Services

Your material, processed to spec. Four dedicated lines handling slitting, blanking, leveling, and cut-to-length under one roof.

How It Works

Toll Processing, Simplified

Ship us your coils, we process them to your specs and ship them back. No inventory risk, no capital tied up in equipment — just precision processing when you need it.

01 — Receive

Your coils arrive at our Carson facility. We inspect, weigh, and log everything before processing begins.

02 — Process

Material runs through our Braner and Stamco lines to your exact width, length, and flatness specifications.

03 — Deliver

Finished product is banded, tagged, and ready for pickup or delivery. Certified weights and dims on every order.

Service 01

Steel Slitting

Our slitting lines cut master coils into narrower strips at precise widths. Whether you need a few strips or dozens per pass, we deliver clean, burr-free edges every time.

  • Width tolerance: ±0.005″
  • Material gauge: 0.010″ – 0.250″
  • Max coil width: 72″
  • Max coil weight: 60,000 lbs

Service 02

Multi-Blanking

Our Braner multi-blanking line produces flat, accurate blanks ready for stamping, forming, or fabrication. High-speed operation keeps your production on schedule.

  • Blank length tolerance: ±0.010″
  • Material gauge: 0.015″ – 0.135″
  • Max coil width: 72″
  • Speeds up to 200 fpm

Service 03

Precision Leveling

Eliminate coil set, crossbow, edge wave, and center buckle. Our levelers produce dead-flat material for downstream processing.

  • Flatness: ¼″ in 12 ft or better
  • Material gauge: 0.015″ – 0.250″
  • Max coil width: 72″
  • Removes coil set and memory

Service 04

Cut-to-Length

Convert coil stock into flat sheets at the precise lengths your production requires. Combined with leveling for flat, stress-free sheets every time.

  • Length tolerance: ±0.030″
  • Material gauge: 0.015″ – 0.250″
  • Sheet lengths: custom to your specs
  • Integrated leveling & stacking
